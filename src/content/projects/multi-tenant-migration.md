Lead engineer at Barracuda Networks.

Identified that the SaaS platform's single-tenant container model was the dominant driver of VM spend; pitched, prototyped, and shipped the migration to a multi-tenant container architecture.

POC → production rollout cut VM cost by ~60% while preserving tenant isolation and per-tenant configurability. Coordinated migration sequencing with platform, SRE, and product stakeholders to keep customer impact at zero.

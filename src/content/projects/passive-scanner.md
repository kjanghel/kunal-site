Designed and built a non-invasive, passive vulnerability scanner that probes prospective-customer sites with curl-class requests, DNS / NS lookups, and other safe surface checks to surface an initial security-posture report.

In production use by sales engineering as the opening artifact in customer conversations, accelerating qualification and giving SEs a concrete entry point.
